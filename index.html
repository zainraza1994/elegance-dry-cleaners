Our Story

Clapham's most trusted name in garment care

For over three decades we have served the South London community with the same commitment to excellence — treating every garment as if it were our own. Whether you're a long-standing regular or new to the area, you'll find the same personal service and uncompromising quality that has defined us since day one.

Our service is grounded in three principles, experience, quality and trust. Our clients trust us with their everyday essentials to their most treasured pieces. We ask questions, we listen, and we do the work properly — that is what has kept people coming back for decades.

Meet the Owner

Habib

With 35 years of experience in high-quality tailoring and dry cleaning, Habib has built Elegance Dry Cleaners into one of the most respected names in South London.

Over the years, he has built relationships not just reputation. Clapham is his community, he is known personally by hundreds of loyal clients, they send their families and friends. He knows them by name. That means something to him.

Habib brings genuine expertise, meticulous attention to detail, and a personal touch to every garment that comes through the door. From a loose button to a beloved wedding gown, nothing is too small or too precious.

Even if you are new to the area, come in and introduce yourself. You will be treated like a regular from day one.

Our Services

✦

Dry Cleaning

Professional cleaning for everyday suits, coats, dresses, and formal garments. Each piece is treated individually with the care it deserves.

✦

Tailoring & Alterations

Expert alterations, repairs, and bespoke tailoring. From taking in a waist to relining a jacket — done properly, by hand.

Wedding Dress Care

Specialist cleaning and preservation for bridal gowns. We understand what these garments mean and handle them with the utmost care.

Leather & Suede
